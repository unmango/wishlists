Which icon goes on the home screen?

Five rough options in the app's indigo and tomato red. Tap one to see it on a light and a dark home screen. Each shows the crops phones apply: Android may cut it to a circle, squircle or teardrop; iPhone uses a rounded square. The dashed ring is the safe zone everything must fit inside.
